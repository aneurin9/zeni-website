Good to know

A few helpful things to say

During setup, you can ask in your own words. These examples can help.

Tips and troubleshooting +
“I’m frustrated”Tell me when something feels unclear or overwhelming so I can help you take the next step.
“Show setup progress”Ask for a picture of your setup progress and what’s left.
“Get me unstuck”Use this if something seems technically stuck or unable to continue.
You can also say things naturally, such as “pause here”, “go back” or “I already did this”. If you need a person, contact the support team below.
