3 handy commands to remember
"I'm frustrated" I'll slow down and break the step down, or connect you with the team if it's something they need to look at. Use it when a step feels unclear or overwhelming, say the word and I'll slow things down.
"Show setup progress" I'll show your current phase, % complete, and exactly what's left. Use it any time you want a clear picture of how far you've come and what's still ahead.
"Get me unstuck" I'll protect your place, work out the safest next step, and help you continue without starting over. Use it only when something seems technically stuck, glitching, or unable to continue.
