FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

Do I need any business experience to use Zeni?
+
No. Zeni is specifically built for people who are new to running a business. You don't need to know business terminology, how taxes work, or how to register a company. That's exactly what Zeni is there for — it explains things in plain English and walks you through every step.
Do I need to download a new app?
+
No. Zeni works through WhatsApp — the app already on your phone. You message Zeni just like you'd message a friend. There's no dashboard to learn, no login to remember, and nothing to install.
What kind of businesses is Zeni for?
+
Zeni is built for service-based businesses (freelancers, coaches, consultants, agencies) and ecommerce businesses (online stores, product sellers, Amazon sellers). If you sell something in Canada, the U.S., or both — Zeni is built for your situation.
What does "guided setup" actually mean?
+
We work with you to get your business properly structured before you start selling. That includes registering your business, setting up your Canadian and/or U.S. entities, getting payment processing in place, and configuring Zeni with your specific business details. It's done with you — not dumped on you to figure out alone.
What's the difference between Core and Pro?
+
Core gives you guided setup and the full Zeni AI assistant — everything you need to start and run your business day to day. Pro adds our team handling your annual tax filings, business renewals (like your LLC annual report or corporate return), GST/HST filings, and ongoing compliance management. With Pro, the admin side of your business is completely off your plate.
Can Zeni help me sell in both Canada and the U.S.?
+
Yes — this is exactly what Zeni is designed for. We help you set up properly in both countries during setup (a Canadian corporation or sole proprietorship + a U.S. entity like a Wyoming LLC if needed), and Zeni tracks your CAD and USD revenue separately, monitors your tax thresholds in both countries, and keeps you compliant on both sides of the border.
What if I already have a business?
+
No problem. If you're already registered and operating, the setup phase gets Zeni configured to your existing business — your entities, clients, filing dates, and financial history. You don't need to start from zero to use Zeni.
