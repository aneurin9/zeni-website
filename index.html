FAQ

Questions I hear a lot.

Do I need any business experience to use Zeni?+

No. I’m built for people who are new to running a business. You don’t need to know the terminology or how taxes work — I explain everything in plain English, at your own pace.

What kind of businesses is Zeni for?+

I’m built for service-based businesses (freelancers, coaches, consultants, agencies), ecommerce businesses (online stores, product sellers, Amazon sellers), and digital businesses (SaaS, info products, digital services). If you’re running or starting an online business — with or without U.S. ambitions — I’m the right fit for you.

Do I need to download a new app?+

No. I work entirely through WhatsApp — the app already on your phone. Nothing to install, no dashboard to learn. You message me like you’d message anyone.

Where are you currently available?+

Ontario, British Columbia and Alberta today, with more provinces on the way. If you’re elsewhere, join the waitlist during Get Started and I’ll let you know the moment we expand.

What if I already have a business?+

No problem — setup gets me up to speed on your existing business: your structure, clients, and financial history. You don’t need to start from zero.

Can I start in Canada and expand to the U.S. later?+

Yes. Most people build a real Canadian business first. When you’re ready, I’ll guide you through U.S. entity setup and cross-border operations — you don’t need to do everything at once.

What's the difference between weekly and monthly?+

Both give you the full Zeni Core experience, including guided setup and your AI business operator. Weekly is CAD $29 per week with a smaller upfront commitment. Monthly is CAD $99 per month and better value for ongoing use.
