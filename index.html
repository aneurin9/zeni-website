FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

Do I need any business experience to use Zeni?
+
No. Zeni is specifically built for people who are new to running a business. You don't need to know business terminology, how taxes work, or how to register a company. Zeni explains everything in plain English and walks you through every step at your own pace.
Do I need to download a new app?
+
No. Zeni works entirely through WhatsApp — the app already on your phone. There's nothing to install, no login to remember, and no dashboard to learn. You message Zeni just like you'd message anyone.
Where is Zeni currently available?
+
Zeni currently serves businesses based in Ontario, British Columbia, and Alberta. We're expanding to more provinces over time. If you're based elsewhere and want to be notified when Zeni comes to your province, email us at support@aneurinadvisory.com.
What kind of businesses is Zeni for?
+
Zeni is built for service-based businesses (freelancers, coaches, consultants, agencies), ecommerce businesses (online stores, product sellers, Amazon sellers), and digital businesses (SaaS, info products, digital services). If you're the founder running an online business — with or without U.S. ambitions — Zeni fits your situation.
What if I already have a business?
+
No problem. The setup phase gets Zeni configured to your existing business — your entities, clients, filing dates, and financial history. You don't need to start from zero.
Can I start with a Canadian business and expand later?
+
Yes. Many businesses start in Canada first and expand into the U.S. later. Zeni helps you build a real Canadian business, get operational, and begin generating revenue. When you're ready, Zeni can guide you through expanding into the U.S., setting up cross-border operations, and accessing a larger market. You don't need to do everything at once.
What's the difference between Core and Premium?
+
Core gives you everything you need to start and run your business — guided setup and the full Zeni AI business operator running daily. Most people start here. Premium is an upgrade for when you want the compliance side fully off your plate: taxes prepared and filed, business renewals managed, compliance handled. Premium work is done by our team, not AI.
