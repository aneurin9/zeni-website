# Zeni premium light redesign concept

This branch is a design-only mockup. It does not change `main`, and checkout is intentionally disabled.

## Strategic direction

- Move away from the common dark navy + neon gradient AI landing-page pattern.
- Position Zeni as a calm, capable business operator for everyday entrepreneurs—not as another AI tool.
- Use a warm light canvas, editorial typography, restrained brand colour and selective liquid-glass surfaces.
- Lead with outcomes and emotional relief: momentum, clarity, fewer missed items and less mental load.
- Keep enough explanation for a new category, but progressively disclose detail instead of presenting dense blocks of copy.

## Main conversion changes

1. Outcome-led hero: “Build your business. Zeni keeps it moving.”
2. Interactive hero demo that cycles through tangible work instead of showing a static phone screenshot.
3. Live activity ticker with specific actions and dollar values.
4. Existing feature list consolidated into four understandable outcome groups: Foundation, Operations, Decisions and Growth.
5. “A day with Zeni” sequence so visitors can imagine the product in their routine.
6. Value section sells reduced uncertainty and mental load rather than AI capabilities.
7. Mobile-specific hierarchy, simplified demo and persistent low-friction CTA.

## Glass treatment

The concept uses restrained “liquid glass” around the navigation, hero demo and mobile CTA. It is not used as the entire visual identity. This keeps the design current without making Zeni look like a short-lived UI trend or another generic AI template.

## Production recommendation

Replace the current top demo with a 35–45 second motion-designed product film based on the interactive scenes in this mockup. The film should show user request → Zeni action → business result, with captions and no reliance on audio.
